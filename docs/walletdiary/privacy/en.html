Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Effective: 5 November 2026

Wallet Diary (“the App”) is developed by HUA-CHIEN KAO (“we,” “us”). It is a personal income and expense diary. This policy explains how we handle information.

1. Summary

  • The App has no account system of our own and does not upload your records to our servers.
  • Data is stored on your device by default.
  • If you use iCloud backup, data stays in your own iCloud account (provided by Apple).
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use third-party ads or analytics SDKs to track you.
  • The App is not a bank or payments client, and it does not connect to your bank.

2. Information we process

The App may process information you enter:

TypeContentsRequired
EntriesDate, amount, currency, income or expense, category, noteYour choice
CategoriesName, symbol, order, income or expenseYour choice
SettingsLanguage, home currency, first day of week, appearanceNeeded to use the App

We do not require email, phone number, or another login. We only see messages you send to our support inbox.

3. Where data is stored

3.1 On device (default)

Entries and categories stay on your iPhone or iPad unless you choose otherwise.

3.2 iCloud backup (optional)

If you use iCloud backup, files are stored via Apple iCloud in your private container (iCloud.com.kao.walletdiary). This is governed by Apple’s iCloud Terms and Privacy Policy. We cannot open or browse your iCloud content.

3.3 Export (optional)

You may export CSV or a backup file and keep it wherever you choose. You fully control those files.

4. Permissions and network

The App does not need camera, photos, location, contacts, or notification permission.

To show an approximate amount in your home currency, the App may fetch public exchange rates (currently from open.er-api.com). That request does not include your ledger or identity.

5. Purchases and payments

The App is free to use. “Support the developer” is a voluntary consumable In-App Purchase. You can buy it again. It does not unlock features.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store card numbers. See the Apple Media Services Terms.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your ledger with third parties, except if required by law or to protect our or users’ legitimate interests.

The App currently has no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

7. Children

The App is a personal ledger, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe such data is in the App, contact us and we will help.

8. Retention and deletion

  • You decide how long to keep data.
  • You can delete entries, categories, or all data in the App.
  • Back up before deleting the App.
  • Uninstalling removes on-device data. Manage iCloud backups yourself in iCloud.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export data. Because data lives on your device or in your iCloud, you can edit, delete, or export it in the App, or email us for help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will post a new “Last updated” date here. For material changes, we will explain them in an appropriate way.
