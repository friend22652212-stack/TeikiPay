1. Summary

  • The App has no account system of our own and does not upload your records to our servers.
  • Data is stored on your device by default.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync or backup, data stays in your own iCloud account (provided by Apple).
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use third-party ads or analytics SDKs to track you.
  • The App is not a payments, transfer, or banking client, and it does not connect to your bank.

We do not require email, phone number, or another login. We only see messages you send to our support inbox.

3. Where data is stored

3.1 Local storage (default)

With iCloud off, items and records stay on your iPhone or iPad only.

3.2 iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable “Sync with iCloud,” data is stored via Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud container (iCloud.com.kao.teikipay). It is governed by Apple’s iCloud Terms and Privacy Policy. We cannot open or browse your iCloud content.

3.3 iCloud Drive backup (optional)

If you tap “Back up now,” a file is saved in the 定期 Pay & Do folder in your iCloud Drive, visible in the Files app. You control that file.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your records with third parties except as required by law or to protect our or users’ legitimate rights.

The App currently includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

7. Children

The App is a personal reminder and ledger tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe such data was entered, and we will help address it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • How long data is kept is up to you.
  • You can delete individual records or items in the App.
  • Back up before deleting the App.
  • Uninstalling removes local data. iCloud sync and Drive backups must be managed by you in iCloud or the Files app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export data. Because data lives on your device or in your iCloud, you can edit, delete, or export it in the App, or email us for help.
